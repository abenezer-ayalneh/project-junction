# Support, Trust, Finance, and Vendor Operations

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-028`, `DEC-035`, `DEC-046`, `DEC-047`, `DEC-105`–`DEC-107`, `DEC-133`, `DEC-162`–`DEC-168`

In-app Support Cases are the canonical service channel. Cases may link a Purchase, Vendor Order, Booking, return, dispute, review, Vendor application, moderation action, or privacy request while preserving least-privilege access. Private offering inquiries replace public Q&A.

Operations follow policy snapshots: return/refund then new purchase (no Product exchange); Flexible/Standard Booking cancellation/amendment; failed delivery retry and pickup grace; no-show evidence/contest; delayed Product/Booking earnings release; and restricted suspension that preserves open obligations. Routine cases use playbooks; exceptions require reason, evidence, scope, communication, and appropriate dual control.

Vendor onboarding is self-service application → private pre-approval workspace → review → approval/rejection/restriction. A rejected/suspended Vendor retains only the access necessary for records, appeal, and lawful resolution of existing commitments. Staff substitution is allowed only under the booked Customer’s captured consent rules.
