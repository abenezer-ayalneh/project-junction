# Policy Catalog and Snapshots

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Normative owner:** exact business-policy values and policy snapshot behavior

Every chosen policy version snapshots at Purchase/Booking commitment. It controls eligibility, deadline, refund/fee responsibility, financial posting, earning availability, event/state guard, and required evidence. Later Vendor/Platform edits do not rewrite past commitments.

| ID               | Policy                        | Exact rule / consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POL-CAT-001`    | Allowed catalog               | only low-risk, fixed-price physical goods and non-regulated fixed-duration appointments may be offered; excluded categories never become a workaround fixture or listing state.                                                     |
| `POL-CAT-002`    | Publication review            | new, flagged, or high-impact listing revisions require risk-based review; routine validated revisions may publish; every public revision stays versioned, auditable, and reversible.                                                |
| `POL-GOOD-001`   | Change-of-mind                | Vendor chooses **Standard 7 calendar days** or **Flexible 14 calendar days** for unused goods, measured from verified handoff; no restocking fee; eligible returned goods follow snapshotted logistics/refund rule.                 |
| `POL-GOOD-002`   | Fault protection              | Fault, wrong-item, or material-misdescription claims have a non-optional **30-day** baseline from applicable handoff; accepted later claim may recover from available/future Vendor earnings.                                       |
| `POL-GOOD-003`   | Failed delivery               | one corrected retry within 48 hours; failure continues to return/inspection/refund path.                                                                                                                                            |
| `POL-GOOD-004`   | Pickup                        | 72-hour collection window plus 48-hour grace; then return/inspection/refund treatment.                                                                                                                                              |
| `POL-BKG-001`    | Flexible Booking              | **100% refund at least 24 hours before start; 50% from 2–24 hours; no refund under 2 hours.** Customer may make two amendments before the 2-hour final cutoff.                                                                      |
| `POL-BKG-002`    | Standard Booking              | **100% refund at least 48 hours before start; 50% from 12–48 hours; no refund under 12 hours.** Customer may make one amendment before the 12-hour final cutoff.                                                                    |
| `POL-BKG-003`    | Amendment economics           | atomic replacement allocation; changed/new components current price, unchanged purchase price; failure preserves original.                                                                                                          |
| `POL-BKG-004`    | Staff substitution/disruption | Any-Staff notice allowed; named Staff needs Customer consent or cancel/refund. Vendor-disruption proposals do not consume Customer amendment allowance. Provider cancellation always returns 100%.                                  |
| `POL-BKG-005`    | No-show/earnings              | Proven no-show follows selected Booking template; Staff evidence + Customer contest; Booking earning releases 48 hours after completion/no-show contest.                                                                            |
| `POL-FIN-001`    | Commission                    | Vendor net sale after Vendor discount, before Platform subsidy, excludes delivery; effective-dated global/Vendor rate; largest-remainder rounding.                                                                                  |
| `POL-FIN-002`    | Promotion                     | at most one Vendor coupon then one Platform campaign; distinct funding/budget reservation/snapshot.                                                                                                                                 |
| `POL-FIN-003`    | Fees and earnings             | Junction absorbs ordinary processor/dispute fees; Product release 7/14 window, Booking 48 hours; disputes freeze affected value only.                                                                                               |
| `POL-FIN-004`    | Reconciliation                | provider/ledger mismatch stays case/exception until approved compensating action; no record editing.                                                                                                                                |
| `POL-FIN-005`    | Payout and chargeback         | automatic weekly batches pay eligible above-minimum non-frozen earnings; chargeback defaults to affected Vendor economics, with an auditable Platform override and no arbitrary withdrawal.                                         |
| `POL-ACCESS-001` | Privileged approval           | low-risk actions are audited; medium-risk actions require reason/post-review; high-risk payout, permanent suspension, bulk export, and secret-recovery actions require a distinct maker/checker, evidence, expiry, and denial path. |
| `POL-TRUST-001`  | Review/suspension/dispute     | verified completed-component review; proportionate restricted suspension/appeal; dispute outcomes full/partial/no refund with audit/approval.                                                                                       |
