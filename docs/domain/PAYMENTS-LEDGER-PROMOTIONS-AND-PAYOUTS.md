`INV-PAY-001` requires every economic event to produce balanced immutable `Money` (integer minor unit, explicit currency) postings. A correction is a reversal/new transaction, never an edited balance. Webhooks verify, deduplicate in durable inbox, and reconcile to provider/Purchase/ledger state. Timeouts are unknown/reconcile rather than blind retry.

Promotion: max one Vendor coupon then one Platform campaign; Vendor discount and Platform funding remain economically distinct. Platform budget reserves during valid hold and releases/consumes idempotently. Commission is transaction-only revenue, based on Vendor net sale after Vendor discount, before Platform subsidy, excluding delivery; rate is effective-dated global/default or audited Vendor override, never category-specific; rounding uses deterministic largest remainder. Portfolio receipt is non-tax; Vendor may attach externally issued invoice.

Earnings are pending/available/frozen by affected component. Product releases after snapshotted 7/14 window; Booking after 48-hour contest; later accepted fault claim may recover from available/future Vendor earnings. Weekly automatic payout batches include eligible above-minimum non-frozen balances. Chargeback principal defaults to affected Vendor balance, with audited Platform override. See `POL-FIN-*`.

## Local Phase 04 C06 Purchase split and receipt — 2026-10-13

Purchase commitment clears captured Customer refund liability in the Customer book and posts each original allocation in its actual seller book: processor clearing and Platform promotion expense against Platform commission and Vendor payable. Positive delivery fees have no promotion/commission. Deferred checks require exact posting amounts, currency, source and component/seller dimensions; balanced but unrelated entries cannot prove commitment.

Component refunds retain immutable source funding and stable per-part provider operation IDs. I/O occurs outside transactions; unknown or failed acknowledgments remain pending. Each acknowledged part atomically posts the opposite original vector, so partial provider success does not conceal money while other parts remain pending. Aggregate operation completion requires all parts; source and root principal caps prevent over-refund. Additive Booking capture is tied to its accepted amendment and original rate. Existing legacy standalone facts are preserved without retrospective posting. Earning release/freezes remain C08; reconciliation/payout/fee approval remain C09.

All development and verification are local. [C06 evidence](../quality/PHASE-04-LOCAL-ACCEPTANCE-EVIDENCE.md#p04-c06--purchase-split-and-receipt--2026-10-13).

## Local Phase 04 C08 component availability — 2026-10-13

C08 partitions existing mixed seller payable into pending, available, case-frozen, refund-pending and refunded amounts. Original compact unit funding plus accepted additive Booking captures remains the amount authority. Availability is a classification, not another sale/processor transfer or an editable ledger balance; C06 remains the economic refund/reversal writer. Append-only `earning_revisions` bind every changed classification to an allocation and sequential version, with database validation against authoritative source funding, cases and policy deadlines. Mutation/truncate and forged classifications are rejected. Audit and `EarningAvailabilityChanged` outbox commit with the revision and owning commerce change.

Goods use actual immutable pickup/delivery handoff plus each purchased 7/14-day policy. A delivery fee uses successful handoff plus the longest purchased goods window (derived conservative local rule). Booking completion uses its recorded 48-hour eligibility. No-show preserves the Phase 03 rule: report → contest finalization → another snapshotted 48-hour eligibility delay. Retained cancellation consideration stays pending until current committed service end plus the contest delay (derived conservative local rule). An unfulfilled/uncompleted component cannot release merely because payment is old.

Open goods ReturnCases reserve exact still-unrefunded original units; decline/no-refund restores only the affected value. A timely Booking contest freezes its remaining original/additive value. Accepted pending refund funding is withheld immediately; acknowledged refund ledger postings move that amount to refunded without a second deduction. Later fault claims can freeze currently available affected goods. Unrelated lines, Bookings and Vendors continue normally. These existing Return/Booking-contest participants supply scoped case freezes; formal Dispute/Support/chargeback decisions remain their later owning context, not a new arbitrary Finance or Vendor balance-edit permission.

Worker ticks process at most 100 changed allocations, without unchanged first-page starvation. A scoped no-store Owner read refreshes current authority and exposes only its Vendor/workspace/Location components; it explicitly reports `not_transferred`. Future C09 batch allocation must refresh authority in the same commerce transaction before selecting non-frozen availability. Legacy standalone payments are not retrospectively posted or made payout-eligible.
