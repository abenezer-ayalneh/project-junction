# Scheduling and Booking — Context Glossary

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Glossary only:** canonical language; no requirements or implementation detail.

| Term               | Meaning                                                             | Avoid                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Availability       | candidate time a qualified Staff member may be allocated            | reservation                         |
| Booking intent     | one requested Service Option/time/mode/Staff preference before hold | confirmed Booking                   |
| Booking hold       | temporary authoritative Staff interval allocation                   | calendar event                      |
| Booking            | paid confirmed one-off appointment for one Customer party           | Product Order                       |
| Attendee detail    | optional person information supplied by adult Customer              | child account                       |
| Named Staff        | Customer’s explicit Staff choice                                    | staff preference without commitment |
| Any Staff          | Customer accepts atomic allocation to an eligible Staff profile     | unassigned Booking                  |
| Staff substitution | replacing allocated Staff under applicable consent                  | arbitrary reassignment              |
| Amendment          | proposed change to a confirmed Booking                              | direct edit of Booking history      |
| Attendance         | mode-specific evidence of service occurrence                        | irreversible completion assertion   |
| Waitlist entry     | notification interest with no priority/reservation                  | booking queue                       |
