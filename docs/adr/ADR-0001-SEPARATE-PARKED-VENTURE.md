# ADR-0001: Keep Project Junction Separate from the Parked Venture

**Status:** Accepted  
**Decision:** `DEC-001`  
**Date:** 2026-08-27

Project Junction is a portfolio-first B2C marketplace specification. The earlier verified social-checkout concept remains byte-for-byte preserved and independently gated. Reusing its payment, market, or launch assumptions would create misleading readiness claims. The trade-off is duplicated research later; the benefit is clear commercial and evidence boundaries.
