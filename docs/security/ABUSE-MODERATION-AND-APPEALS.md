# Abuse, Moderation, and Appeals

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Normative owner:** abuse controls and enforcement process  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-046`, `DEC-047`, `DEC-105`, `DEC-106`, `DEC-162`, `DEC-163`, `DEC-166`

The portfolio target prohibits controlled, medical, financial, legal, adult, weapons, alcohol, regulated, and digital goods. Publication is risk-reviewed; prohibited content is blocked or removed with a reason. Private offering inquiries and in-app Support Cases are the sanctioned help channels; public Q&A is excluded.

`POL-TRUST-001` defines warning, restricted suspension, suspension, appeal, and reinstatement. Enforcement must be proportionate, scoped, reversible where possible, evidence-backed, and visible to the affected party except where disclosure creates a safety or fraud risk. A restricted Vendor can preserve records and cooperate in open Orders/Bookings but cannot create new customer-facing commitments. Permanent consequences and high-risk reinstatements follow dual control.

Reviews are tied to completed eligible Purchases/Bookings, have one author per eligible component, use anti-retaliation/privacy checks, support a Vendor response where allowed, and enter a moderation/appeal lifecycle. Abuse signals may trigger manual review; they do not autonomously confiscate earnings or make legal conclusions.
