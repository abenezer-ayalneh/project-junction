# Public Demo Description

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Normative owner:** public portfolio-demo promise  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-003`, `DEC-004`, `DEC-107`, `DEC-109`, `DEC-139`, `DEC-172`, `DEC-175`–`DEC-178`, `DEC-180`

The planned public demo is a reviewer-facing, no-signup experience that proves the portfolio system’s flows with clearly synthetic people, Vendors, content, payments, verification, meetings, notifications, and operational activity. It is not a marketplace launch, a payment service, an identity-verification service, or evidence of Dire Dawa commercial readiness.

A reviewer may safely switch among Customer, Vendor Owner, Staff, Support, Trust & Safety, Finance, and Platform Owner personas. Each demo workspace expires exactly 24 hours after creation, is quota-limited, uses a non-guessable isolated namespace, and is automatically purged. Role switching must expose only synthetic data appropriate to that persona. It may never use a production credential, real payment, identity document, Vendor application, or customer record.

The demo must visibly state its synthetic/sandbox status, share an evidence guide, model mixed goods-and-Booking checkout, returns/support/moderation/financial operations, and include accessible short public videos only where captions or no-speech declarations are present.
