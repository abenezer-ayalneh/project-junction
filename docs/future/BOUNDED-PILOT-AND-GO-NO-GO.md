# Bounded Pilot and Go/No-Go

**Status:** Requires future authorization and validation  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-001`, `DEC-109`, `DEC-120`, `DEC-153`

A future pilot, if authorized, must have a finite duration, explicitly recruited and consented cohort, limited categories/Vendors/Locations, no-exceptions operating hours, payment/legal approvals, monitored success metrics, safety/escalation paths, customer remediation, data-minimization plan, stop triggers, and an exit/deletion plan. It must not begin as an unbounded public beta.

The go/no-go meeting receives evidence from every commercial launch gate and records: scope, counterfactual/no-go option, residual risks, owners, funding, provider/operations readiness, customer/Vendor communications, data retention, and explicit approval. Failure to meet a metric or safety threshold is a no-go unless a revised bounded plan is separately approved.
