# Dire Dawa Research Plan

**Status:** Requires future validation; no field research, Vendor recruitment, or commercial outreach has occurred.  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-001`, `DEC-011`, `DEC-109`, `DEC-180`

The portfolio uses synthetic Dire Dawa context only. A future team must independently research customer demand, Vendor segments, delivery/pickup feasibility, service scheduling norms, address/landmark usability, device/connectivity constraints, accessible language needs, payment preference, unit economics, and operating support capacity. Start with ethical, consent-based desk research and interviews only after explicit authorization.

Outputs are dated `REF-*` evidence, assumptions, sample methodology, limitations, raw-data privacy plan, and a decision log. No generic Ethiopia fact, portfolio visualization, or sandbox flow is enough to infer city-level readiness. Research may recommend no launch.
