# Vendor Demand, Logistics, and Unit Economics

**Status:** Requires future validation  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-027`, `DEC-054`, `DEC-055`, `DEC-131`, `DEC-132`, `DEC-161`, `DEC-179`, `DEC-180`, `DEC-182`

Research must test whether the selected low-risk product categories and service categories have sufficient Vendor and Customer demand; whether Vendor-managed pickup/delivery milestones/proof are viable; how address/landmark and Location zones work in practice; what failed-delivery retry and pickup grace cost; and whether support, refund, fraud, and payout-delay policies are understandable and economically sustainable.

The unit-economic model must separately expose Customer price, delivery fee, discount funding, ordinary processor/dispute fee absorbed by Junction, Vendor commission/earnings, return/refund loss, support/moderation cost, provider cost, and failure-rate sensitivity. The US$25 third-party-service ceiling is a portfolio budget constraint, not a commercial cost forecast. No claim of viability is made until representative evidence and pilot results exist.
