# Rejected, Deferred, and Superseded Choices

**Document status:** accepted baseline  
**System claim:** specified

## Rejected in Release 1

- C2C selling, shared canonical catalog, backorders, quote jobs, rentals, home service, group classes, recurring appointment series, Staff-specific service pricing, rooms/equipment capacity, two-way calendar sync, recording/transcription, Product exchanges, public Q&A, general social inbox, platform fleet/carrier, Driver dispatch, live GPS, cash on delivery, tax calculation, wallet balances, escrow claims, arbitrary Vendor withdrawals, cross-border commerce, digital goods, and regulated/high-risk categories.

## Deferred

- Native applications.
- Human-reviewed local translations.
- Bounded assistive AI features.
- Non-cash expiring loyalty points.
- Chapa after Stripe sandbox/Connect.
- Generalized resource reservations, calendar write/sync, video/recording products beyond the selected public-media scope, and real commercial launch.
