# Audience, Personas, and Jobs

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-003`, `DEC-008`–`DEC-010`, `DEC-107`, `DEC-164`, `DEC-168`–`DEC-173`

## Primary audience

Technical hiring teams are the primary audience. They need credible breadth, honest boundaries, accessible product thinking, and implementation-ready decisions—not market-hype claims.

## Product personas and jobs

| Persona            | Job to be done                                                              | Essential experience                                                                              |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer           | find a reliable good/service and complete it without juggling sellers       | discovery, clear availability/policy, one mixed Cart, receipt, support/dispute/return path        |
| Vendor Owner       | offer goods/services and operate one business storefront                    | self-service application, private setup, catalog, Locations, staff, fulfillment, finance insights |
| Vendor member      | perform assigned catalog, fulfillment, schedule, or finance work safely     | preset least-privilege role and optional Location scope                                           |
| Staff              | deliver a booked service without handling unrelated business administration | availability, attendance/no-show evidence, consent-aware substitution limits; optional User link  |
| Platform operator  | resolve marketplace exceptions transparently                                | scoped Support/Trust/Finance/Vendor-ops queues, reason/evidence/audit and dual control            |
| Portfolio reviewer | assess the system without exposing personal data or spending money          | no-signup synthetic demo, role switching, scenario guide, evidence links                          |

An adult account holder may book for a minor attendee, but is the contracting account holder and is responsible for account actions. There are no child accounts, group-seat sales, C2C sellers, or real demo personas.
