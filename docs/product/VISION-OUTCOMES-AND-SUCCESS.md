# Vision, Outcomes, and Success

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-002`–`DEC-005`, `DEC-014`, `DEC-112`, `DEC-153`, `DEC-157`–`DEC-160`

## Vision

Demonstrate that one thoughtfully bounded marketplace can handle the hard seams between goods commerce, appointment allocation, multi-Vendor checkout, payment accounting, support/trust, and production operations. It is deliberately broad in capability but not broad in operational ambition.

## Portfolio outcomes

1. A technical reviewer can trace a public feature to requirements, policy, state machine, API/event contract, test, and future evidence.
2. A reviewer can safely explore Customer, Vendor, Staff, and Platform workflows without real accounts, money, or identity data.
3. Engineering claims are specific: correct Cart/Checkout holds, Staff allocation, ledger/reconciliation, permission boundaries, recovery, and accessibility—not generic “full-stack” language.
4. No public messaging claims real payment processing, provider approval, market validation, or commercial readiness without evidence.

## Success measures for a future implementation

Success is evidence, not polished screenshots alone: all release gates pass, the public demo proves its stated mixed-goods-and-Bookings scope, ASVS 5.0 Level 2 applicability has evidence, recovery and provider-failure exercises have evidence, and a reviewer can follow the guide unaided. This is solo, milestone-driven work; there is no artificial deadline. A feature is not “done” merely because its UI renders.
