# Product Description

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Normative owner:** what Project Junction is and what its actors experience  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-001`–`DEC-015`, `DEC-157`–`DEC-160`, `DEC-168`–`DEC-180`

Project Junction is a portfolio-first, B2C multi-Vendor marketplace for physical goods and fixed-duration appointments. “AliExpress-level” describes breadth of marketplace capability—not traffic, catalog scale, global logistics, staffing, legal coverage, or commercial readiness. Its primary audience is technical hiring teams who should be able to inspect credible end-to-end product, engineering, security, financial, and operations reasoning.

A Customer can discover products/services, buy goods, book one-person appointments, use a mixed multi-Vendor Cart, select pickup or Vendor-managed delivery for each goods group, and attend in-person or online services. A Vendor Storefront can sell products, services, or both. Platform operators handle support, trust, finance, moderation, and Vendor operations. The first public portfolio release must include the complete mixed experience—not an earlier goods-pickup-only slice.

The portfolio target uses synthetic Dire Dawa context, ETB display, Ethiopian address/phone conventions, and `Africa/Addis_Ababa`. It is not a live Ethiopian marketplace. The separate parked social-checkout venture remains independent at [verified-social-checkout-parked.md](../ventures/verified-social-checkout-parked.md); no commercial approval or payment assumption transfers between them.

## Integrity promises

- Every publicly claimed feature has a complete normal, failure, security, accessibility, financial, support, and operational story.
- Vendors are contracting sellers; Junction models the payment-merchant role only in the selected Stripe sandbox unified-charge target. This is not a live commercial assertion.
- Delivery means Vendor milestones and handoff proof, not Junction fleet dispatch, driver management, route optimization, or GPS tracking.
- The public demo is synthetic, no-signup, role-scoped, quota-limited, and expires after 24 hours.
- Any Dire Dawa commercialization is separately gated by [future launch gates](../future/DIRE-DAWA-COMMERCIAL-LAUNCH-GATES.md).
