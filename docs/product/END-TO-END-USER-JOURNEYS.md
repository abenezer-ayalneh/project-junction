# End-to-End User Journeys

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-018`–`DEC-041`, `DEC-053`–`DEC-055`, `DEC-125`, `DEC-131`–`DEC-133`, `DEC-157`–`DEC-168`, `DEC-181`–`DEC-189`

## Customer mixed commerce journey

1. A Guest searches typed Product/Service verticals, then signs in with a verified adult account before checkout.
2. The Customer chooses up to five independent appointment intents plus products from several Vendors; each product group has one eligible fulfillment Location and delivery/pickup selection.
3. Checkout creates an all-or-nothing expiring stock/Staff hold and displays snapshotted price, policy, delivery, promotion, and availability details.
4. Verified sandbox payment creates one Purchase, Vendor Orders for goods, confirmed Bookings for services, ledger events, notifications, and later eligible earnings—not a real-money claim.
5. Goods advance through pickup-ready/collected or Vendor delivery milestones/proof. Bookings advance through in-person or online attendance/completion, with no-show/dispute path.
6. The Customer uses a Return Case/refund then a fresh Purchase rather than an exchange, or opens a Support Case/dispute with scoped evidence.

## Vendor and Staff journey

1. A business applies, receives a private pre-approval workspace, completes permitted setup, and is approved/rejected/restricted through reviewed operations.
2. Approved Vendor members create listings, Location inventory, zones, Staff qualifications, schedules, policy choices, and catalog imports.
3. They prepare paid goods, record delivery/pickup proof, or deliver scheduled work. Staff may be substituted only according to booked Customer consent.
4. They view their scoped operational/financial data; they cannot unilaterally alter ledger history or escape policy/approval controls.

## Platform journey

Support, Trust, Finance, and Vendor Operations receive scoped cases/events, apply policy snapshots, collect evidence, take dual-controlled high-risk action, and retain audit/reconciliation history. An outage, timeout, duplicate callback, or failed handoff opens a safe recovery path rather than inventing success.
