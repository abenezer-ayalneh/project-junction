# Seed Categories and Synthetic Content

**Status:** Specified — Not Executed — Not Verified  
**Decision coverage:** `DEC-011`, `DEC-046`, `DEC-139`, `DEC-168`–`DEC-180`

The balanced synthetic category set is:

- apparel and accessories;
- phone and computer accessories;
- home and everyday goods;
- grooming appointments;
- tutoring/coaching appointments; and
- photography/creative appointments.

Fixtures should demonstrate simple/variant goods, inventory Locations, pickup/delivery zones, in-person/online services, named/any Staff allocation, booking amendment/cancellation/no-show, returns/disputes/support, reviews, and financial reconciliation. Every name, Vendor, address, phone/email, image, document, payment, meeting, and notification must be synthetic or properly licensed fixture material.

Seed content is not a demand forecast, endorsement, cultural claim, real Vendor recruitment, or a basis for commercial selection. No controlled or excluded categories appear even as a demo fixture. Demo data follows its own expiry/purge boundary and cannot be promoted to another environment.
